## Checklist

### Stage layout and proportions
- [x] **Four-sided stage in the middle of the field.** A raised deck inside a runway ring with rounded corners. `nest_首场_0001`, `nest_526_0001`.
- [x] **Runway ring = the 舞台 box on the seat map.** The A-zone floor blocks run the same length as the ring, so the ring is the 57 × 36 m stage footprint already in `venues.js`. `nest_首场_0001`, `nest_全集视角_0001`.
- [x] **LED volume is a box with a 2.77 : 1 long face and a 1.53 : 1 end face.** Long face is about 0.7 of the ring length. Built as 40 × 22 × 14.4 m. `nest_首场_0001`, `nest_526_0004`.
- [x] **Six truss towers carry the roof**, three along each long side, just inside the LED volume. `nest_大对角_0006`, `nest_大对角_0003`, `nest_526_0004`.
- [x] **During the show the LED volume is flown** with its top at the roof and the stage open below it. `nest_226_0001`, `nest_917_0002`, `nest_大对角_0006`.

### LED volume
- [x] **Black wall, 1.0 × 0.5 m cabinets visible up close.** `nest_526_0004`, `nest_917_0001`.
- [x] **Thin lit frame around each face.** `nest_537_0006`, `nest_537_0009`, `unknown_全价位_0007`.
- [x] **Before the show every face carries the 万兽之王 title on black.** `nest_526_0004`, `nest_917_0001`, `nest_718_0009`.

## Not done

- **Split LED mode.** In some songs the volume separates into an upper and a lower band (`nest_大对角_0003`, `nest_226_0011`). The model keeps one flown box.
- **See-through LED mesh.** Dark content lets the towers show through the wall (`nest_226_0008`, `nest_大对角_0006`). The model wall is opaque.
- **Lion and eagle.** They are real (`nest_首场_0002`, `nest_首场_0003`) but are not in the mascot list, and they stand in the audience.
- **Seated floor crowd.** Floor blocks are seated on chairs (`nest_526_0001`, `nest_801_0005`). The model still shows standing people.
- **Roof height and rake.** Photos do not give metres. Tier heights are unchanged; see `BOWL_PARAMS.md`.
- **Daylight look.** The model is night only.
